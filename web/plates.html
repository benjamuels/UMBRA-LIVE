UMBRA LIVE · PLATES

Every plate there is, to watch, try out and choose from. The FM-1's bank holds sixteen, and CORONA is always there besides. Pick a plate to play it and turn its controls; drag the ones you want into the loading bank below, then connect the FM-1 and load them. Loading stops playback and pauses the sound for a moment, so do it between sets. Under the plates are the palettes their colours come from.

PALETTES

The FM-1 keeps four palettes of six colours and uses one at a time. A plate's parts take their colours from the one in use: which part takes which is under COLOURS in the player. Pick a palette, then a colour, to change it. A plate playing here follows at once.

GENERATE

Plates add these colours as light over black, so dark ones barely show. SAT and LIGHT set how much the six differ.

FROM AN IMAGE

Six colours that stand apart, taken from a picture and laid out by hue. Drop one here or choose a file. It stays in this browser.

IMPORT / EXPORT

What you set here is for trying the plate out: it is not sent to the FM-1. There, the same controls are on HOME > UMBRA > LIVE VIZ CTRLS (the elements with an ON switch under the eight there too), and the parts' colours on HOME > UMBRA > COLOR.
