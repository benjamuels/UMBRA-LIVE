UMBRA OBSCURA

Plates: load plate files into the FM-1's plate bank. CORONA is built into the firmware; every other plate lives in the bank, and SELECT in TOTALITY steps through what is loaded. Loading stops playback and pauses the sound for a moment, so do it between sets.

The bank uses the flash where projects 5 to 8 were kept (UMBRA keeps four projects). Loading a plate overwrites what was stored there: back up from the editor first if you had songs in projects 5 to 8.
