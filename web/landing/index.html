The instrument underneath

Built on JIANT FM

Umbra Live is JIANT FM with a different skin and a live-visuals system added. The sound engines, the sequencer, the effects, songs, saving and MIDI are all JIANT, the work of Juan Jiant, a muralist and visual artist from Buenos Aires. JIANT is built on Hügelton Instruments' Felucca. Umbra Live replaces one thing, HOME's ecosystem of creatures, with plates you play, and adds the pages to perform them.

To learn the instrument itself, JIANT's own site is the place to go. If Umbra Live makes something with you, support the person who made what's underneath it.

Three steps, no coding

Everything runs in the browser. You don't need to install a program, and you can play Umbra Live before touching your FM-1.

01

Try it in the browser

The emulator runs the real Umbra Live firmware on your computer or phone, with sound and the plates. Press PLAY, then tap HOME on HOME for TOTALITY.

  • No FM-1 needed
  • The same plates, layers and pages as on the FM-1
Open the emulator
02

Install it on your FM-1

Open the installer in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable, press Install and wait without unplugging. The plates are written with it.

  • It checks your FM-1 first and saves a backup
  • It's JIANT's installer, so it says JIANT in places
Open the installer
03

Edit, back up, add plates

JIANT's web editor shows your sounds and settings while the FM-1 keeps playing. The plates page sends plates into the FM-1's plate bank.

  • Back up every project with one click
  • A bank of 16 plates; the install fills 11
Open the editor

Needs: Chrome or Edge on a computer (WebUSB and Web MIDI) and a USB cable that carries data, not only charge. Projects 5–8 hold the plate bank in Umbra Live: back up any projects saved there before you install. Coming from another custom firmware (Felucca, baudgirl or any other)? Return to official V15 first, with the installer's button.

What Umbra Live adds

One eclipse, twelve plates

Every visual is drawn by the music. A note lights something at its pitch, in its track's colour; the kick, the snare and the hats each do their own thing. Between notes the picture rests, so the screen never takes time from the sound.

Aplates

12 generative plates

Fields of light (CORONA, CINDER, GRANULE, TARN), string art (SAROS, FILAMENT) and six in line art (BEACON, ZODIAC, SONAR, MOTHS, MARSH, PACHINKO). True black, high contrast, a colour for each track.

See them all ↓
Blayers

A ground and three layers

One plate fills the screen as the ground; up to three more lie over it. The line-art plates, SAROS and FILAMENT can sit on a layer, and so can BODIES and BEINGS.

Cfull screen

TOTALITY

Tap HOME on HOME and the picture takes the whole screen: no tabs, no numbers. The keys, knobs and sequencer keep working, so you play the set from inside it.

Dpictures

LIVE VIZ CTRLS

Every plate is made of parts, called elements. For each one, pick the tracks whose notes drive it and link it to anything that moves: KNOB 1–4, a track's hits, LFOs, steps, the level, the beat or a tap.

Esound

LIVE AUDIO CTRLS

Four macros, M1–M4, sent to JIANT's modulation matrix. In TOTALITY they sit on KNOB 1–4, so one turn can move both the sound and the picture.

Ffigures

BODIES and BEINGS

BODIES gives each sound engine an astronomical figure in place of JIANT's creatures: ORBEATRY for the drums, DIAMOND RING, GEMINIDS, COMET and more. BEINGS brings JIANT's own per-track beings back as a layer.

+underneath

All of JIANT's instrument

Six tracks, JIANT's synthesis engines (ANALOG, FM6 with DX7 banks, PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, NOISE, ANIMAL, DRUM-X …), the 64-step sequencer with MUTATE, effects, songs and the generator, USB audio out and MIDI. They're all there and they work as JIANT describes.

ALGORITHM picks the page: each plate, BODIES, BEINGS, SCREEN and EFFECTS. KNOB 1 picks an element in the left column, and its live bar shows what it's doing. KNOB 2 picks a control on the right:

Where
OFF, GROUND or LAYER 1–3.
Set
The element's value at rest.

Which tracks drive it (KNOB 3 the track, KNOB 4 on or off). None means AUTO, the plate's own rule.

Where it came from

An AV set on one instrument

The idea: write, prepare and play an AV set on one small instrument, with nothing else on stage.

JIANT had already turned the FM-1 into a complete instrument. Its HOME screen is an ecosystem of little creatures, one for each track, breathing with their own audio. They're charming, but Umbra Live leans towards work that is moody, abstract, ambient and minimal. So the plan was to keep all of JIANT and turn that screen into generative art made for the FM-1's 240 × 240 panel and its limits.

The first plate came from paketa12's TouchDesigner patch, a reaction–diffusion "lava" rebuilt in fixed-point code for the FM-1. It redrew the whole screen every frame, which was too heavy, so lighter plates took its place. They kept its one rule: the picture moves because you play.

Umbra Live is the first full version: twelve plates, layers, the controls rebuilt so every part of a plate can be played, and the engines' figures redrawn as bodies in the sky.

Everything in the name comes from the eclipse.

Umbra
The darkest part of a shadow, where you stand at totality.

On a bigger screen

The projector

The projector draws a plate as large as a browser window, played live by the notes your FM-1 sends. Put it on a projector or a second screen.

Open the projector
  1. On the FM-1, set MENU › CONTROL › MIDI OUT to ALL.
  2. Connect it by USB and open the projector in Chrome or Edge (Safari has no Web MIDI).
  3. Press Start and let the browser use your MIDI devices.

The page is silent and only draws. You pick its plate and settings on the page; the FM-1's own aren't sent.

FAQ

Before you install

Is this JIANT?

It's JIANT FM V10.7.6 with HOME's visuals replaced, as an independent fork. It isn't an official JIANT release, and JIANT's author doesn't support it, so please never send Umbra Live problems to him. If something seems wrong, check whether it happens on JIANT too.

What's left out from JIANT?

To make room: HOME's ecosystem and the engines' creatures (BODIES and BEINGS take their place), projects 5–8 (their flash holds the plate bank), USB audio input, USB host and the USB serial console. USB audio out, stems, USB-MIDI and Bluetooth MIDI are all there.

Will I lose my sounds or projects?

Back them up with the editor before installing or changing version. Projects 5–8 are where the plate bank lives, so anything saved there is replaced. Coming from Felucca, samples USR1–3 are lost on install, as with JIANT.

Can I go back to JIANT or the official firmware?

Yes. The installer has a button to return to the official V15 firmware (you choose the official FM-1.fwsc from M-VAVE), and it saves a complete backup first. From there you can install JIANT from its own site.

Which browser do I need?

Chrome or Edge on a computer for the installer, the editor, the plates page and the projector: they talk to the FM-1 over USB and MIDI. The emulator also works in other browsers and on phones.

The installer doesn't find my FM-1.

Use a USB cable that carries data (many only charge), connect it straight to the computer without a hub, and close other programs that use the FM-1 over MIDI.

Is it safe?

It's at your own risk, and young. Umbra Live is tested in the emulator and built on every change, but it hasn't had JIANT's years of testing on real FM-1s. Back up first, and never write it straight over another custom firmware: return to official V15 first. That step has saved FM-1s from being bricked.
